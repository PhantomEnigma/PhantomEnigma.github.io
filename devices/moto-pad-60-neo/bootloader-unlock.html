ZUI Firmware

Bootloader Unlock Guide
Moto Pad 60 Neo

⚠️ Warning & Disclaimer

  • Data Loss: Unlocking the bootloader automatically performs a full factory reset. Back up your photos and important files standard.
  • OTA Updates: Unlocking may permanently disable official over-the-air (OTA) updates.
  • Warranty: Proceed entirely at your own risk.

🧩 Prerequisites

💻 PC / Laptop with ADB & Fastboot drivers configured
🔌 High-quality USB data cable
👤 An active Lenovo Account

⚙️ Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Enable Developer Settings

Configure your tablet software permissions before connecting to PC:

  1. Unlock Developer Options: Go to Settings → About Tablet, scroll to the bottom, and tap Build Number 7 times until a toast appears saying "You are now a developer!".
  2. Enable OEM Unlocking: Navigate back to Settings → System → Developer Options, locate OEM Unlocking, and enable the toggle switch.
  3. Enable USB Debugging: In the same Developer Options menu, scroll down to USB Debugging and turn it ON. Confirm the pop-up prompt on your tablet screen.
2
Retrieve Tablet Identifiers

Connect your tablet to your computer via USB cable. Open your command prompt or terminal and execute:

1. Retrieve Device Serial Number (SN)

adb shell getprop ro.serialno

2. Retrieve Bootloader Serial Number (bootload_sn)

adb shell getprop ro.boot.bootload_sn
Save these values: Copy both text strings returned by the terminal and paste them into a text document. You will need them for Step 3.
3
Request & Download Unlock File
  1. Apply on ZUI Portal: Visit the official Lenovo ZUI Unlock Portal and log in with your Lenovo account.
  2. Submit Device Information: Select the category containing y900 11&13. Input your SN, bootload_sn, and your email address, then solve the captcha and submit.
  3. Download Image File: After ~10 minutes, open our Lenovo SN Downloader Tool, enter your bootload_sn, and download your sn.img unlock file.
4
Flash Unlock File & Finish
  1. Reboot to Bootloader: Run the following command or reboot manually holding Volume Up + Power: 
    adb reboot bootloader
  2. Flash Unlock File: Open terminal in the folder where sn.img is stored and execute: 
    fastboot flash unlock sn.img

    Then execute:

    fastboot oem unlock
    ⚠️ Confirm on Device: Immediately press the Volume Up button on your tablet when the prompt appears on screen.
  3. Verify Unlock Status: Check that bootloader unlocking succeeded (should return unlocked: Yes): 
    fastboot getvar unlocked
  4. Wipe Device & Reboot: Perform a factory reset: 
    fastboot -w

    Reboot into system:

    fastboot reboot
🎉 Your bootloader is now successfully unlocked!
